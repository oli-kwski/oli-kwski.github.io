What Are Private Endpoints and Why You Should Use Them

When you spin up an Azure Storage account, an Azure SQL database, or a Key Vault, those services are — by default — reachable over the public internet. They’re protected by authentication and firewall rules, but the traffic still traverses Microsoft’s public endpoints. For many organisations, especially those with compliance requirements or a zero-trust security posture, that’s not good enough. Private Endpoints solve this.
